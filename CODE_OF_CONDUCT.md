# Code of Conduct

This project is a collaborative space for improving the ADLC Manifesto and
related open source materials.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas, wording, implementation, and evidence.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and
  perspectives.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Using project spaces to promote unrelated products, services, or agendas.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the
project or its contributors.

To report a concern, use the repository's private reporting tools when
available or contact the maintainers privately.
